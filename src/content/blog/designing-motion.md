## Feedback

Immediate feedback for actions such as pressing a button or submitting a form should be perceived within roughly 100 milliseconds.

## Transitions

Changes in interface state need continuity. Translation and opacity can establish a clear spatial relationship between elements.

## Narrative

Hero motion can express a brand's character, but it should be used sparingly and offer a static alternative for people who prefer reduced motion.
